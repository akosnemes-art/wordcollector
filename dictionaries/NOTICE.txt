From the repository root, regenerate the asset with Python 3.9 or newer.
python3 tools/build_wiktionary_dictionary.py hungarian.jsonl hu_50k.txt en_dbnary_ontolex_2026-11-01.ttl.bz2 dictionaries/wiktionary-hu-en.json.gz

Live lookups use Wiktapi and return Wiktionary contributors’ data.
https://wiktapi.dev/quickstart
